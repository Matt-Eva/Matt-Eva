# Matt Eva

Hello there! I'm Matt. I'm a software developer, field service engineer, writer, and former educator.

I started programming about 5 years ago, driven by a desire to build my own publishing platform for writers. That drive has fueled my learning, and has served as the foundation for much tinkering and many deep dives - NoSQL databases, systems architecture and design, scalability, various language paradigms (and tradeoffs), and, of course, the pragmatic lessons that all programmers must learn, namely reliability, maintainability, and extensibility.

Currently, I'm building in parallel using two seemingly polar paradigms - Ruby on Rails and Go. I love both, and I appreciate both - right now I'm investing more back in Go, and implementing lessons I've learned over the past few years of experimentation. 

I also love to write and teach. As someone who is largely self-taught, I'm always looking to help demystify technical concepts and connect people with useful tools and ideas. For me, learning and teaching are two sides of the same coin - when I'm doing one, I'll want to do the other.

When I'm not coding, writing, or working, you can find me playing music, wandering over hills and under trees, reading, and learning new things. I'm always interested in new opportunities and meeting new people, so if I seem like someone you'd like to connect with please reach out!

Happy coding!
