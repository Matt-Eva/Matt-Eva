# Matt Eva

Hello there! I'm Matt. I'm a software developer, field service engineer, writer, and former educator.

I started programming about 5 years ago, transitioning from a career as a content writer and editor, and driven by my desire to build my own publishing platform for writers. That drive has fueled my learning, and has served as the foundation of much tinkering and many deep dives - NoSQL databases, systems architecture and design, scalability, various language paradigms (and tradeoffs), and, of course, the pragmattic lessons that all programmers must learn, namely reliability, maintainability, and extensibility.

Currently, I'm building in parallel using two seemingly polar paradigms - Ruby on Rails and Go. I love both, and I appreciate both - right now I'm investing more back in Go, and implementing lessons I've learned over the past few years of experimentation. 

I also still love to write and teach, and contribute in any way toward demystifying technical concepts and connecting people to useful or paradigm shifting tools and ideas. For me, learning and teaching are two sides of the same coin - when I'm doing one, I'll want to do the other too.

When I'm not coding, writing, or working, you can find me playing music, wandering over hills and under trees, reading, and learning new things. I'm always interested in new opportunities and new connections, so I seem like someone you'd like to connect with please reach out!

Happy coding!
